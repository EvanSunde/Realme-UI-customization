Installs over 0.9.x and 0.8.0. Check that the APK's signing certificate matches the fingerprint at the bottom of these notes.

## New (experimental)
- **Music widget** (Extras → Music widget). This is realme's own lock-screen player: cover art, title, artist, and previous/play-pause/next buttons. You can drag and resize it in the expanded preview.
  - **It needs the Studio Music Bridge test app.** No app on the phone provides the music service this widget reads from, so the bridge app pretends to be that service.
  - **The bridge is a separate test APK.** It's signed with a development key and isn't part of this release.
  - **Without the bridge,** the widget shows "Nothing playing".

## How to test the music widget
1. Install the Studio Music Bridge test app and open it once. Its status line should say exactly 1 service answers the music action.
2. In Studio, turn on Extras → Music widget, then apply.
3. Lock the phone. The widget should show "Bridge test song 1" with a coloured cover.
4. Back in the bridge app, check the log. It lists every call the lock screen made.
5. Try **Play / pause** and **Next song**, and the widget's own buttons.
6. If the cover is missing, pick another "Cover file" option in the bridge app and lock the phone again.
7. Then tap **Follow what's playing**, allow notification access, and play something in Spotify or YouTube Music.
8. Use **Copy log** in the bridge app and send the log back.
