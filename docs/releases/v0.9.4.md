Installs over 0.9.x and 0.8.0. Check that the APK's signing certificate matches the fingerprint at the bottom of these notes.

## Removed
- **The experimental music widget (0.9.2–0.9.3).** Together with the Studio Music Bridge test app, it crashed the lock screen, and the phone fell back to the stock lock screen. This release is 0.9.1 again, with only the version number changed.

## After updating
1. Uninstall the **Studio Music Bridge** test app.
2. Open Studio and apply your lock screen again. That replaces the theme that still contains the music widget.
