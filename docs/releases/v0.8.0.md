## ⚠️ Uninstall 0.7.0 (or older) first
From this release on, builds are signed with a new private key. Android won't install 0.8.0 over an older build.
1. In the old app, open **Scenes → Backup → Export all** to save your designs. Photos have to be picked again after importing.
2. Uninstall the old app, then install `Lockscreen-Studio-0.8.0.apk` from the assets below.
3. Open it, grant Shizuku once, and import your backup.

Future updates will install normally over 0.8.0. Check that the APK's signing certificate matches the fingerprint at the bottom of these notes.

## New
- **Full-screen preview.** It fills the screen in your phone's real shape.
  - The clock moves with your finger from wherever you grab it, instead of jumping to your touch point.
  - Pinch to resize and twist to rotate, or use the corner handle with one finger.
  - Size goes up to 600 and rotation the full ±180°.
- **Separate hours, minutes and date.** Split the clock into three pieces, then move, resize and rotate each one on its own. Not available for Grid, Tower, Edge, Arc or Analog.
- **6 new layouts:** Analog (hands move with the live time), Big minutes, Diagonal, Wave, Offset and Wide.
- **10 new finishes:** Outline glow, Two-tone stroke, Retro 3D, Embossed, Chrome, Holo, Split tone, Halftone, Glitch and Stencil (the wallpaper shows through the digits).
- **Colour suggestions** from your photo and your wallpaper, for both the hours and the two-tone minutes.
- **Theme Store:** works with `com.oplus.themestore` and `com.heytap.themestore`.

## Fixed
- Scrolling past a slider no longer changes it by accident, and tapping a slider bar no longer makes it jump.
- Pop-up messages no longer block touches on the preview.
- The default caption is now empty.
- Removed "Use current Material You colour"; use the photo or wallpaper suggestions instead.
