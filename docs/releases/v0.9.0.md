Installs over 0.8.0. Check that the APK's signing certificate matches the fingerprint at the bottom of these notes.

## New
- **Stretch.** Drag the side handle to make the clock wider, or the bottom handle to make it taller. Condensed fonts (Bebas, Anton, Barlow Condensed) stretch best.
- **Per-part styles.** In Style, choose All, Hours, Minutes or Date, then give that part its own colour, font, weight and finish. It works whether or not the clock is split.
- **Move the date on its own** in every layout.
- **Day counters** (up to 3) and a **daily quote** from your own list. Both can be moved and resized.
- **Charging styles:** glow pulse, colour shift, battery fill, charge ring or energy bar.
- **Rings** layout, and a **seconds hand** for Analog.
- **Depth:**
  - choose which digits sit in front of the subject
  - the subject casts a soft shadow onto the clock
  - rim light in the clock colour on the subject's edge
- **Photo fill:** your photo shows through the digits.
- **Weight through the hour:** the digits get bolder every 10 minutes.
- **Parallax:** the clock and the photo drift apart. It can follow tilt, a sideways drag, or both.
- **Lock-screen shortcuts** (up to 4): apps, camera, ringer (silent/vibrate/normal), Wi‑Fi and mobile data. They show their live state, with an optional vibration on tap.
- **Alert badges** for missed calls and unread messages. Each appears only when there is something to show.
- **Light mode colours** that switch with the phone's light and dark theme.
- **Water ripple:** the photo or gradient background ripples where you touch it.
- **Shade colour suggestions** from your wallpaper, your photo and your clock.

## Fixed
- **Breathe and Float now move.** They used animation tags that Realme's engine ignores.
- **24-hour clock after midnight.** With the phone set to 12-hour time, it showed 12:xx between 00:00 and 00:59. The day/evening/night switch was also wrong in that hour.

## Removed
- Fuzzy, Word grid, Roman, Binary and Nepali digit layouts (only Rings was kept).

## Please test on the phone
The theme syntax was checked against Realme's lock-screen engine (`com.oplus.uiengine`), but these haven't been seen on a real lock screen yet:
- **Shortcuts:** the camera should open over the lock screen. Other apps should ask you to unlock first.
- **Ringer, Wi‑Fi and data:** each toggle should switch, and its icon should follow.
- **Alert badges:** with Google Phone or Messages, the counts may stay at zero.
- **Charging styles:** plug in and unplug.
- **Light mode colours:** switch the system theme.
- **Water ripple, Parallax drag, Breathe and Float.**
- **Photo fill:** does the texture line up with the photo behind it?
- **Digit depth, shadow and rim light.**
