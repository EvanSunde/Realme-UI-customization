## Music widget test (with Studio Music Bridge 0.2)
The widget is at **Style → Information → Music widget**.

1. Update the bridge to 0.2, open it once, and keep "Reconnect after every change" on.
2. Turn the widget on in Studio and apply.
3. Lock the phone. You should see "Bridge test song 1" and its cover.
4. On the lock screen, tap the widget's play and next buttons. The title and cover should change.
5. In the bridge app, tap **Copy log** and send it back.
