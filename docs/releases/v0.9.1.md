Installs over 0.9.0 and 0.8.0. Check that the APK's signing certificate matches the fingerprint at the bottom of these notes.

## Fixed
- **Shortcuts now respond to taps.** Realme's lock-screen engine only counts a "click" if your finger lifts within 0.1 s, so most taps were ignored. Shortcuts now fire when you lift your finger, as long as it barely moved, so a swipe that starts on an icon still unlocks instead.

Reapply your lock screen after updating so the fixed shortcuts are written into the theme.

Everything else from 0.9.0 still needs testing on the phone; see the [0.9.0 notes](https://github.com/EvanSunde/Realme-UI-customization/releases/tag/v0.9.0).
